# Surface Brief: index.html

- **Scope & Mode:** Single-page interactive mobile/web puzzle duel (`operate`).
- **Audience & Job:** Friends on Android phones (APK or mobile web) racing to solve identical seeded polyomino boards against an hourglass via P2P room links (`?room=CODE`), plus solo training.
